Terms of Use

The App is for personal study.

2. Pricing

"Premium" is available as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly ¥300, or yearly ¥2,000 with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time purchase (¥5,000, no renewal). Every option unlocks the same content. Prices include tax; the App Store display takes precedence.

3. Unofficial app

This is an unofficial study app for people preparing for the Japanese security guard qualification test (traffic guidance, grade 2, written part). It has no connection with the National Police Agency, any prefectural public safety commission, or any other public organization. The questions are original practice questions written from published laws and government documents; they are not actual test questions. Passing is not guaranteed. The content is based on the laws as of 2026-11-23 and may differ from current rules after amendments. First-aid content is for study only and is not medical advice. In actual security work, follow the latest laws and your employer's instructions.

All questions, explanations and summaries are original works of the developer. No past test questions, no questions recalled by examinees, and no text, figures or photos from textbooks or the resuscitation guidelines are included. The laws and documents used as the basis are shown with each question and under "Sources" in the App.
